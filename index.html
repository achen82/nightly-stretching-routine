Using it on iPhone

Add to Home Screen: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then “Add to Home Screen.” It will launch full-screen like an app.

Keep it on screen while you stretch. The timer asks iOS to keep the display awake (Wake Lock) while it runs. Sounds and voice cues are reliable only while the app is in the foreground with the screen on — iOS can suspend web apps in the background or on the lock screen, so lock-screen alerts aren’t guaranteed. If the app does get backgrounded, the timer pauses itself rather than losing your place.

Technique & safety

  • Don’t roll directly over the back of the knee, kneecap, Achilles tendon, IT band, or prominent hip bone.
  • Glute work stays on muscle tissue with comfortable pressure. Stop for sharp pain, numbness, tingling, or pain shooting down the leg.
  • Avoid forceful calf stretching if it causes Achilles insertion pain. Every stretch should feel gentle, never painful.
  • This is a mobility and comfort routine, not treatment for an injury — worsening or persistent pain warrants proper evaluation.
